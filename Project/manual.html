Your three global animation drivers

dayPhase +0.0028 / frame
Feeds sin() inside getDaylight(). One full cycle is 2π ≈ 2244 frames ≈ 36 seconds.
cloudShift +0.0008 / frame
Horizontal offset for cloud layer. Wraps +2.6 → −2.6 for seamless loop.
flagPhase +0.08 / frame
Time term in flag ripple sine. Much faster than dayPhase — cloth flutters.
